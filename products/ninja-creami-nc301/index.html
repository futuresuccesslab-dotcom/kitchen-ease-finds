Ninja CREAMi NC301: seven frozen treats in one machine

The Ninja CREAMi NC301 processes a fully frozen base into ice cream, gelato, sorbet, milkshakes, smoothie bowls and other frozen treats. It includes seven one-touch programs and two 16-ounce pint containers with storage lids.

Why it may be useful

  • Seven programs cover ice cream, gelato, sorbet, milkshake, smoothie bowl, lite ice cream and mix-ins.
  • The two included pints allow separate flavors to be prepared and stored.
  • The Mix-In program distributes additions such as fruit, cookies, nuts or chocolate through the frozen base.
  • Removable pints, lids and processing components simplify routine cleanup.

Things to consider

  • Most recipes require the prepared base to freeze for about 24 hours before processing, so this is not an instant ice maker.
  • Processing a fully frozen pint can be noticeably loud for a short period.
  • Accessories are model-specific; confirm that extra pints are compatible with the NC300 series.
  • Follow the manufacturer's fill lines, assembly instructions and care guidance.
