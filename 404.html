Why it may be useful

  • Eleven programs cover scoopable and drinkable frozen treats, including ice cream, gelato, sorbet, milkshakes and frozen drinks.
  • The two 24-ounce XL tubs provide more capacity for family-size batches and allow separate flavors to be prepared and stored.
  • Dual processing options let you process the top and bottom portions separately or prepare the entire tub.
  • Removable tubs, lids and processing components simplify routine cleanup.

Things to consider

  • Most recipes require the prepared base to freeze for about 24 hours before processing, so this is not an instant ice maker.
  • Processing a fully frozen tub can be noticeably loud for a short period.
  • Accessories are model-specific; confirm that extra tubs are compatible with the NC500 series.
  • Follow the manufacturer's fill lines, assembly instructions and care guidance.
